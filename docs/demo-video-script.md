# BrandProof demo video — 2:35 master script

This master fits the Perfect Corp 1–3 minute requirement and the Nutrient/Xano 2–4 minute requirements.

## Shot list and narration

### 0:00–0:18 — Problem and outcome

Show the live hero and integrated-provider strip.

> Beauty teams launch from scattered specifications, certificates, price sheets, market tabs, and email approvals. BrandProof turns that mess into one governed path from evidence to a shopper-ready experience—without publishing a claim nobody verified.

### 0:18–0:42 — Xano operating backbone

Scroll through the five gates and point to the policy gate.

> Xano is the operating backbone. It owns the data model, business rules, provider credentials, state transitions, and audit receipts. The browser never sees a sponsor key, and each stage remains locked until the previous gate passes.

### 0:42–1:10 — Nutrient extraction

Run the launch, then show the five extracted fields and their pages.

> We start with a three-page synthetic product dossier. Nutrient DWS performs the core document operation and returns five page-grounded claims. Notice the intentional conflict: the marketing sheet says SPF 50, while the laboratory certificate supports SPF 30. BrandProof does not average or guess.

### 1:10–1:32 — Human authority

Show the decision reason and approve SPF 30.

> A person chooses the authoritative evidence and records why. Only after we approve the laboratory-supported SPF 30 claim does Xano unlock current market research. This is a real control, not a decorative human-in-the-loop label.

### 1:32–1:54 — SerpApi live market evidence

Run the market scan and show all three populated results.

> SerpApi supplies structured, live Google Shopping context: current products, prices, and retailers. BrandProof keeps this evidence visibly separate from certified product truth, so a search result can inform a launch without rewriting the record.

### 1:54–2:17 — Perfect Corp consumer experience

Configure try-on and show the rendered fictional portrait.

> Once evidence and market gates pass, Perfect Corp creates the consumer experience. Xano lists an available AI Look template, submits our permission-safe fictional portrait, polls the asynchronous task, and accepts only a successful HTTPS render.

### 2:17–2:35 — Proof and close

Scroll to the receipt table and final approval gate.

> The result is a 100-percent-ready workflow with seven live receipts across Xano, Nutrient, SerpApi, and Perfect Corp. Publication still requires explicit operator approval. BrandProof is evidence before experience—and a better operating system for product launch.

## Recording notes

- Record the public URL in live mode at 1440×900 or 1920×1080.
- Keep all sponsor status badges and the receipt table legible.
- Do not display provider dashboards, credentials, browser history, or developer tools.
- Use a fresh workflow and pause after each live response so judges can read the result.
- End on the enabled `Request final approval` button without clicking it.
